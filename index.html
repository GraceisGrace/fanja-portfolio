Meet Fanja

I’m a woman who is passionate about community development and humanitarian action. And what matters most to me is helping vulnerable people live with dignity. I care deeply about community health, nutrition, and safe water and sanitation, with a strong focus on gender equity, youth leadership, and protecting every child’s right to thrive.

I’ve had the privilege to design and coordinate programs with organizations like Save the Children and Catholic Relief Services.
Moving from specialist to coordinator roles taught me how to turn strategy into action: managing complex projects, supporting teams, and adapting solutions to local realities.
My background blends social sciences and a Master’s in Private Law, with technical training that keeps me close to the field.

My goal is to lead high-impact programs that address both urgent needs and long-term resilience , I want to design interventions that respond to nutrition and health emergencies, while also strengthening community autonomy through sustainable, rights-based approaches. For me, success is when communities have the tools and confidence to drive change themselves.
